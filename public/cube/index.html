          How to play

          One look, one chance. The clock is your score.

          • A cube of blocks shows for a few seconds. Memorize it. The clock starts the moment it appears.
          • Then it's gone. Tap a spot to stack blocks: 1, 2, 3, then empty again.
          • Tap Check. All green and the clock stops. One pink spot and it's over for today.
          • Sure of it early? Tap I'm ready to save seconds. A new cube every day: easy on Monday, very hard by Sunday.
